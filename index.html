Privacy Policy

So Easy - Monthly Bill Tracker · Effective 3 October 2026

In short: your bills never leave your phone. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I do not collect, store or share any of your information.

This policy explains how the "So Easy - Monthly Bill Tracker" app for Android (the "app") handles information. The app is published by SALSA Apps ("I", "me").

What the app stores

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • The bills you add: name, amount, optional due day, and whether you have marked it paid this month.
  • Your settings: currency symbol, pay date, and light/dark appearance.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to me, to any server, or to anyone else.

What the app does not collect

  • No account, name, email address or other personal details.
  • No location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no third-party advertising or analytics code.

The app works without an internet connection.

Permissions

The app uses your phone's vibration only to give a small tap of feedback when you mark a bill as paid. It does not request any other sensitive permissions.

Sharing your information

I do not sell, share or disclose your information, because I do not have it. The app is distributed through Google Play, and Google's own privacy policy applies to your use of the Play Store.

If the app offers an optional paid upgrade, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. I never see or store your payment details.

Device backups

Depending on your phone's settings, Android may include app data in your device backup to your Google account. This is controlled by Android and Google, not by the app or by me.

Keeping your data safe

Your bill data is protected by Android's app sandbox, which stops other apps reading it, and by your device's lock screen. I recommend keeping your phone locked and up to date.

Deleting your data

You can edit or delete any bill inside the app. To remove everything, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app. Because I hold no copy of your data, nothing is left behind.

Children

The app is a general budgeting tool and is not aimed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, I will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@salsa-apps.co.uk.
